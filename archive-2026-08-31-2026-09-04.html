Pagination fix not fully resolved — blog category pages

Action needed
What we found

Pagination enhancements were implemented across the blog category pages (People, Tech, Models, Heritage, Travel) to surface the full article list instead of the previous 10-article limit. SEO has since flagged that the Models category — the site's strongest traffic driver — still isn't displaying correctly: pagination is present, but page 2 onward isn't showing the remaining 140+ articles.

Next step

Investigating and correcting the pagination behaviour on the Models category as a priority, given its traffic significance for SEO.

Subaru Somerset West missing from AutoTrader

Action needed
What we found

Both the AutoTrader API investigation and the separate pre-owned API integration work confirmed the same root cause: Subaru Somerset West (Dealer ID ATU7SSW00) has not been added as a dealer on AutoTrader's side. This isn't something we can trigger or fix from our end — new dealers and integration updates need to be actioned directly by AutoTrader.

Recommended action

Follow up directly with AutoTrader to request the Somerset West listing be added — looping in the Dealer Principal, who has offered to assist, may help move this along. Once added, we'll retest immediately to confirm stock is syncing and displaying correctly.
