SubaruSA — Dev

This week, the email routing changes confirmed in the Master Forms Sheet were applied to the live website forms, starting with Subaru Durban after a Book a Test Drive lead on 2 November went to the previous contact. Work continues on the forms register change alerts, the Pre-Owned lead form fix, the priority article image fixes, the technical SEO fixes and the MY26 Outback product page updates, with the Outback 360° viewer ready for Robin's updated images.

Section 03 · Detail

Task details

1

Form Email Routing Changes — Applied

Done
The email routing changes confirmed in the Master Forms Sheet needed applying to the live website forms. Subaru Durban was the priority, after a Book a Test Drive lead on 2 November went to the previous contact instead of the new Dealer Principal.

Applied the confirmed Add and Remove changes from the Master Forms Sheet across the live forms, starting with Subaru Durban. Durban's Sales, Love Our New Standard and Referral forms now route to the new Dealer Principal, and the Pre-Owned form routes to the Dealer Principal and the Pre-Owned contact. Rows with no action selected were left as they were, as they are still unconfirmed.

Next steps: please confirm the West Rand Service Booking recipient, as one address is marked as both "Remove" and "Add" in the sheet — it has been left as is until confirmed. On the Pre-Owned form, please let us know who should receive Centurion leads, as no replacement recipient has been given.

Master Forms Sheet
2

Book a Test Drive Forms — Model Field Added

Done
A Book a Test Drive submission for Subaru Durban on 2 November came through without the vehicle model, and the selected model needed to be captured and shown on every lead.

Investigated the missing model on the Durban submission and added a Model selection field to all the Book a Test Drive forms across the website, so customers choose the vehicle they are interested in. The selected model now comes through in the email notification when the form is submitted.
3

Homepage Banner — BirdLife SA Option C Built

Awaiting approval
Following the client's approval of Option C for the BirdLife SA video banner, the banner was to be built for the homepage with the wording updated from "SA Bird Life" to "Bird Life SA".

Built Option C for the homepage banner, using the BirdLife SA video as the reference, and updated the wording to "Bird Life SA".

Next step: the banner has been shared for review. Once approved, it will be taken live.
Option C — built banner

Forms Register Change Alert Automation

Pre-Owned Lead Form & Dealer Routing Fix

In testing
The Pre-Owned lead form had issues with tracking, vehicle identification and lead routing. Scope covers a separate Pre-Owned version of the New Sales Leads form so leads can be cross-checked with the dealerships, a vehicle field so each lead shows the vehicle of interest, adding the form to the master forms register, and routing leads to the correct dealerships, with tracking coordinated so the Contact Us and Pre-Owned forms can be distinguished.

The fix has been implemented and is in testing. The Durban Pre-Owned routing has been updated from the Master Forms Sheet, and the Centurion recipient is still to be confirmed.
