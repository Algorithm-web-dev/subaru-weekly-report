SubaruSA — Dev

A big week for client-facing launches. The Subaru events are now fully live on both the homepage and the dedicated events page, with homepage buttons pointing straight through. Subaru East London's Maintenance Campaign form now routes sales leads directly to Ricky Montgomery in Port Elizabeth, reflecting East London's shift to a service-only centre. The Maintenance Plan / "Fall in Love with Peace of Mind" campaign dates have been updated across all ad platforms and creatives to show the 31 July offer end date. The Referral Programme page has a full content refresh — new heading, intro copy, a new section for the personalised leather laptop bag, and updated reward messaging — all QC'd and live. We also fully resolved the recurring issue where the client's notification email wasn't going through on the briefing form, with a full structural rebuild to make the process easier and more reliable for the client going forward. On CFAO, a session with Tinus walked through the lead reference numbers, field mapping, and call routing — everything is confirmed correct on the CMS side, and we've been given the green light on the lead flow; going forward Tinus is available directly for any dealer queries. Web Dev 2/2, the campaign removal, and the broader website refresh remain on track for their scheduled dates.
