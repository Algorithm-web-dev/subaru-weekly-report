Section 03 · Detail

Task details

1

Black November — Deal Assist Updates Live

Live 30 October
The Deal Assist updates for the Black November promotion, focused on the Forester. The offer was revised from R80,000 to R50,000 during the week, with updated creative supplied.

Updated the Deal Assist page to feature only the Forester, replaced all Deal Assist pop-ups with the latest Black November creative for desktop and mobile, removed the Crosstrek pop-up so the pop-up appears only on the Forester page, and updated the homepage banner with the link kept to the same destination. The changes were shared on staging for review. Following the client's feedback, the "Select Your Model" field was removed, as the page now features one vehicle, and the T&Cs copy was updated on the live form at go-live. The client approved, and the updates went live on 30 October.

Deal Assist page

Homepage Banner Video & BirdLife SA Update

Awaiting client
The client asked to replace the current homepage banner with a video. Mock-ups showing how the YouTube video would look and play in the banner have been prepared and shared for review, along with the wording update from "SA Bird life" to "Bird Life SA".

Next step: please confirm which mock-up you prefer, and it will be built out.

Book a Test Drive Form — Vehicle Model Tracking

In progress
The vehicle model is not coming through on some Book a Test Drive submissions, so sales consultants cannot see which vehicle the customer is interested in. Scope covers tracing the affected submission using the supplied ID, checking the form across all vehicle pages, fixing whatever is preventing the model from being captured, and testing on multiple vehicle pages to confirm the correct model comes through with every submission.

In progress

Pre-Owned Lead Form & Dealer Routing Fix

In progress
The Pre-Owned lead form has issues with tracking, vehicle identification and lead routing. Scope covers a separate Pre-Owned version of the New Sales Leads form so leads can be cross-checked with the dealerships, a vehicle field so each lead shows the vehicle of interest, adding the form to the master forms register, and routing leads to the correct dealership addresses once confirmed. Tracking is being coordinated so the Contact Us and Pre-Owned forms can be distinguished.

Section 04 · Quality assurance

Issues found & fixes

!

Vehicle model missing from Book a Test Drive submissions

Action needed
What we found

Some Book a Test Drive submissions are coming through without the vehicle model, so sales consultants cannot see which vehicle the customer is enquiring about.

Next step

The affected submission is being traced and the form checked across all vehicle pages, so every submission carries the correct vehicle model.

!

Pre-Owned leads not reaching dealerships

Action needed
What we found

The Pre-Owned lead form is grouped with the Contact Us form in tracking, the vehicle of interest is not captured correctly, and leads are not reaching the dealerships. An estimated 319 leads may be affected.

Next step

A separate Pre-Owned form with a vehicle field is being set up, with tracking coordinated so the two forms can be told apart. Please confirm which dealership email addresses should receive Pre-Owned leads so routing can be configured.

!

Forms register change alerts not yet triggering

Action needed
What we found

Changes made to the live email list did not send a notification to the team, so the automated change alerts are not yet working as intended.

Next step

The alerts are being finalised and tested alongside the Register Your Interest form updates from the client's feedback, so the team is notified whenever the register is updated.

!

Pagination fix not fully resolved — blog category pages

Action needed
What we found

Carried over. Pagination enhancements were implemented across the blog category pages (People, Tech, Models, Heritage, Travel) to surface the full article list instead of the previous 10-article limit. SEO flagged that the Models category — the site's strongest traffic driver — still isn't displaying correctly: pagination is present, but page 2 onward isn't showing the remaining 140+ articles.

Next step

TO CONFIRM — current status. If still open, correcting the pagination behaviour on the Models category remains a priority given its traffic significance for SEO.

!

Maintenance run — outstanding items

Action needed
Plugin licenses still missing

Carried over — 3 plugins remain outdated pending license keys: Advanced Custom Fields PRO, WP All Export Pro, and WP All Import Pro. Please share current license keys so these can be updated on the next run.
