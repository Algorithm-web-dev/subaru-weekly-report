SubaruSA — Dev

This week, following the client's request for more vehicle information on Pre-Owned leads, the stock ID, colour, price and other key vehicle details were added to the Pre-Owned lead form, as the CMS does not accept links — so dealers now receive the vehicle details with each lead. The West Rand and Centurion form recipients were also updated. Work continues on the MY26 Outback product page, the forms register change alerts, the priority article image fixes, the technical SEO fixes and the article consistency fixes. The second CRO round and the second maintenance run, including form email testing, follow later this month.

Section 03 · Detail

Task details

1

Pre-Owned Lead Form — Vehicle Details Added

Done
Following the rebuild of the Pre-Owned lead form, the client asked for more information on the vehicle to be sent to the dealers with each lead. The CMS does not accept links in the lead, so a link to the vehicle on its own was not an option.

Added the key vehicle details to the Pre-Owned lead instead — including the stock ID, colour and price — so the dealers receive the information they need on the vehicle the customer is interested in. The form has been tested and is working.
2

Form Routing — West Rand & Centurion Recipients Updated

Done
Following last week's routing changes, two recipients were awaiting the client's confirmation: the West Rand Service Booking recipient, and who should receive Centurion Pre-Owned leads.

With the recipients confirmed, the routing has been updated on both forms.

Master Forms Sheet

Outback Exterior Images — Updated

On staging

Issues found & fixes

Form recipients awaiting confirmation

Resolved
What we found

Fix

With the recipients confirmed, the routing on both forms has been updated.
